# Features (summary of the upstream README)

Prospect discovery (DuckDuckGo/Bing/Google, optional paid sources), owner and email finding with verification, signals (facts collected only after you confirm), cold-email writing with strict rules, Outbox approval, reply handling and meeting booking, compliance footer, sending limits and quiet hours, warmup readiness check, local dashboard (Today, Signals, Companies, Contacts, Campaigns, Outbox, Conversations, Activity, Usage, Settings), CSV export, optional Codex brain (not on Windows). Details: `app\README.md`.
