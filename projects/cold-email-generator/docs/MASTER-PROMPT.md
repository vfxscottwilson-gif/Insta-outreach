# Cold Email Master Prompt
Paste this whole file into a new chat together with a new lead sheet. The workflow below is followed exactly, in this order.

---

## 0. WORKFLOW (never skip)
1. Analyze the sheet: columns, niches, angles, content mix, name quality.
2. Show a PREVIEW first: one full 5-email sequence (email + 4 follow-ups) per niche, built from real rows. Build nothing else.
3. Wait for my "go ahead". Never generate the full batch before that.
4. Build the file, run the rule checker, have a second reviewer read a sample, then hand over the file with the check results.
5. Always state done / not done / not verified.

## 1. WHO I AM (value, never "a tool")
I am a person who grows businesses. I edit video and run social media, ads and branding for creators and restaurants. The services are the means; the result is more clients and more diners. Experience: over seven years (written out, never with a plus sign). Use "over seven years" in only 2-3 emails per batch.

Rotate the value angle across the batch, never the same one in every email:
- I have helped businesses like yours grow (no revenue numbers, no invented case studies)
- Every cut or campaign is a human judgment call, built for the viewer or diner I want
- The services are the means, the result is clients or a busier dining room
- Short years-of-experience line (limit above)
Services line stays ONE short line (restaurants: Meta and Google ads, SEO, Reels, design, branding, websites. YT: edits, motion graphics, thumbnails, plus Shorts only if the channel posts Shorts).

## 2. OUTPUT FILE
Tab "Ready to Send", one row per lead, columns in this order:
Email | First Name | Subject Line | Email Body | Score | Follow-up 1 | Follow-up 2 | Follow-up 3 | Follow-up 4 | Why this score
Tab "All Leads": every input row with STATUS (PASS/FAIL) and REASON.
Font Arial 11 on every tab. Sorted by score, highest first. Follow-ups are sent as replies in the same thread (no separate subject).

## 3. FILTER AND SCORE
- Drop: no valid email, duplicate email, no-reply/bounce addresses.
- Restaurants: lead_score >= 60. YT: every valid email stays.
- Score out of 10 = the ICP score already in the sheet divided by 10 (size fit, budget, activity, money signals). YT: minus 1 if the channel already credits an editor. "Why this score" explains it in one line.

## 4. SUBJECT LINES
- At least 30 characters; first letter capital, rest lowercase; no symbols; never cut a word to fit, go a few characters over instead.
- Personalized to the person, channel or restaurant. Never generic ("quick question").
- Format "Name + hook" in at most 10% of rows, never consecutive, one every ~10 rows. Check that name plus hook reads as a sensible sentence. If the name is long, use the plain subject.

## 5. EMAIL BODY RULES (first email and every follow-up)
- First letter capital. Only commas, periods and question marks. No + ; : = | & * or em dashes. Hyphens only inside words (30-60, long-form).
- Services as a short line ("Meta and Google ads, SEO, Reels..."), never "including X:" with a colon.
- Short, breathable: one idea per line, blank line between paragraphs. First email about 75-100 words. Follow-ups 40-70 words.
- Never the same sentence in every email: rotate opener, value line, sample line, CTA, sign-off. A sentence may not repeat inside one lead's sequence.
- Professional, never offensive, never telling someone their channel or restaurant is weak. Numbers like a low engagement % are never quoted; turn them into an opportunity.
- Channel and business names: split camelCase, "&" becomes "and", strip emojis and odd characters, "AI" stays capitals. If a name cannot be cleaned safely, use a neutral phrase ("your channel").

## 6. THE SAMPLE (YouTube)
30-60 seconds is the LENGTH OF THE EDIT I deliver. The prospect sends any raw clip (any length) from one of their videos; I cut a free 30-60 second sample. Every sample line must contain "30-60 second" and "free" or "no cost" / "zero cost" / "no charge". Never "Want to see it?" as the only ask (it implies the sample already exists). Never "send me a 30-60 second clip".
Restaurants: I offer a free sample (a short Reel or graphic) directly, completely free, no raw clip needed.

## 7. PITCHING THE RIGHT THING (do not repeat the offended-reply mistake)
- Read each lead's content mix before choosing the angle. A long-form-only creator is NEVER pitched Shorts or "clip your content". The pitch is long-form retention: pacing, hooks, story order, thumbnails.
- Low-engagement and low-frequency leads open with their strengths (subscribers, views per video), then the opportunity.
- Do not claim facts the sheet does not show.

## 8. FIRST NAMES (never guess)
- Use a first name only if it is in a real first-name dictionary AND the channel name is a person-shaped name (two or more words, no digits, no business words, not ALL CAPS, not a single word).
- Single-word channels, brands, merged lowercase names (orenmeetsworld, flippingdrawers), financial/tech brands: "Team".
- Restaurants: always "Team" (the sheet has no confirmed owner names).
- To find a real owner name manually: search the contact email on Google plus "who is the owner of this YouTube channel". Found: use the owner's first name. Not found: "Team".

## 9. FOLLOW-UP SEQUENCE (every lead gets all four)
Every follow-up is personalized (name, channel or restaurant, a real detail), 40-70 words, a different angle, never repeating a line. Touches 1-3 contain the free sample line with "free" and "no cost" / "zero cost"; touch 4 does not.
- Follow-up 1 (+5 business days): refers to the first email, light "you are probably busy, popping back once" nudge, restates the free sample, asks for a "yes".
- Follow-up 2 (+10): a different angle (asks what matters to them, or another way I can help), sample stays on the table, ends with one easy question.
- Follow-up 3 (+15): frictionless one-number reply. "Just reply with a number." then three lines starting "1. ", "2. ", "3. " (a dot and a space), medium length each: send the free sample / check back next month / not a fit. Appreciate any reply.
- Follow-up 4 (+20): last touch, graceful exit, no sample line. Says it is the last follow-up in fresh words, assumes timing not rejection, door stays open, seasonal good wish.
Wording rotates between three variants per touch. Seasonal lines follow the current month (written for November, holiday season); regenerate for another month.

## 10. QA BEFORE HANDOVER
Run the rule checker on the file (symbols, capital letters, subject length and share of "+" subjects, sample wording, follow-up word counts, numbered options, FU4 wording, odd first names) and have a second reviewer read a sample for sense, tone and offence risk. Report the results with the file.

## 11. SECURITY
No keys, passwords or API tokens in any file or chat. Emails are only sent after I approve the send.
